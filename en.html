Privacy policy for PureRecord

Updated: 29 October 2026

PureRecord is an Android app by MK Forge Games for recording, playing, trimming and sharing screen videos and audio. Recording and editing work locally and offline. The app has no user accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. PureRecord has no internet permission and does not upload your recordings to a server. Optional feedback and bug report forms open separately in your browser.

2. Screen and audio recording

In video mode, PureRecord processes the screen content you authorize through Android. Depending on the Android version and your choice, this is the whole screen or a single app. This content may include personal information.

Audio mode saves sound only, as M4A or MP3. You choose device audio, microphone or both. Android requires audio permission and consent through its system dialog for device audio too. Microphone-only recording needs no screen-sharing consent and processes no screen image. Other apps can block recording of their audio. We do not automatically receive recorded content.

For optional app selection, PureRecord reads the names, icons and identifiers of launchable apps on your device. Your selection stays in private app settings. Device audio can be limited to the selected app; an enabled microphone still records your surroundings. The app list and your selection are not sent to us or external servers.

3. Local storage and deletion

Videos and trimmed videos are saved as MP4 in your chosen local folder under Movies or DCIM, by default Movies/PureRecord. Audio recordings and audio trims are saved as M4A or MP3 in your chosen folder under Music, by default Music/PureRecord. PureRecord reads file names, dates, duration, size and, for videos, resolution and thumbnails. An audio waveform is calculated locally. The app checks free storage. Trimming creates a new file and keeps the original.

Recording options, storage folder, language, notices and optional edge controls are saved in private app preferences. A local recovery log references files that have not been completely saved. PureRecord does not provide cloud backup of these preferences.

Trimming creates a new file and preserves the original. Temporary export files in private app cache are removed after completion or cancellation, or during the next export cleanup. Incomplete recordings may be kept locally for possible recovery; recovery is not guaranteed.

Finalised videos remain until you delete them with PureRecord or another authorised app. PureRecord asks for confirmation before deleting a video. Uninstalling removes private app preferences and cache; videos already saved in public media folders remain on your device.

4. Permissions

Screen sharing enables the particular recording you start. Microphone permission is required when selecting audio; Android also requires it for permitted device audio capture. Notifications provide visible controls for active recordings and exports. Display over other apps is an optional special permission used for the countdown over the selected app and enabled edge controls on supported devices. Without this permission, the countdown can be turned off. The countdown window is removed before the video file begins.

Android's folder picker sets the local storage location. PureRecord does not request all-files access, camera, location, contacts or accessibility permission. Show taps is managed through Android developer options. You may revoke permissions in Android settings; affected features may then be limited.

5. Sharing and other apps

Sharing a video or audio file opens Android�s app chooser. Only the app you select receives read access to that file. Its own privacy policy applies to further processing.

Saved videos may be visible to gallery apps and other services with appropriate permissions. Gallery or device cloud services you have enabled may synchronise these files independently. PureRecord does not perform this synchronisation itself.

6. Optional feedback and bug report forms

Give feedback and Report a bug open Google Forms in your browser after a notice. These forms are optional and unnecessary for recording or editing. PureRecord does not automatically attach videos, logs, device identifiers or diagnostics, or prefill personal information.

We process only what you submit: your message, and for bug reports the steps, expected result and any device model, Android version or app version you enter. An email address for replies is optional. The forms do not automatically collect your Google email address and do not allow file uploads. Do not submit passwords, private recording content or other confidential information.

Google Forms stores submissions in our Google account. We do not publish responses. When accessing this external service, Google also processes connection and usage information such as IP address, browser information and possibly cookies or account information. Google services may process data outside the EU or EEA. Information about Google's processing and described transfer mechanisms is at Google. You may email us directly instead.

We use feedback to answer questions, fix errors and improve the app. We delete personal form responses and associated support messages no later than six months after closing the case, unless statutory retention or necessary legal defence requires otherwise. Fully anonymised bug descriptions and suggestions may be retained for development.

General feedback and bug reports are processed under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is answering requests and providing a safe, functioning app. Statutory retention is based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR. If we request consent for additional processing, you may withdraw it at any time for the future.

The app does not give us access to local recordings. Android permission enables a device feature and does not replace any legal basis required for recordings of other people you initiate.
